Free Stand Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Free Stand is a free, open source app (GPL-3.0). It has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your data.

Your files

Your songs, charts, audio and setlists are stored on your own device.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Free Stand asks for the drive.file permission. This lets the app see only the files it creates, or that you open with it, in your own Drive. It cannot see your other files. The access token is kept only in the running app and is sent only to Google. You can disconnect at any time in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third parties

Links to Buy Me a Coffee open in your browser and are governed by that service's policy.
